Check your dryer

Warning signs your vent is clogged

  • Clothes need two cycles or come out damp
  • The dryer or laundry room gets very hot
  • A burning or musty smell while it runs
  • The outside flap barely opens
  • Lint around the outside vent or behind the dryer
  • It's been over a year since a cleaning

Smell burning right now? Turn the dryer off and don't use it until the vent is checked. If you see smoke or flames, get out and call 911.

Why it matters

Lint builds up where you can't see it

Your lint screen catches most lint, but not all of it. The rest collects inside the vent pipe, where heat from the dryer has nowhere to go.

Questions

Common questions

How often should I clean my dryer vent?

At least once a year. Every 6 months for big families, pets, long runs or roof vents. Clean the lint screen after every load.

How long does it take?

Most homes take about an hour. Roof vents and long runs take longer.

Is my white plastic or foil duct OK?

No. Plastic and thin foil duct can crush, trap lint and burn. Code calls for smooth rigid metal duct, with only a short, visible listed connector behind the dryer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the vent's length, roof access, stories and any repairs. You get a firm price before we start.

Can I do it myself?

Clean your lint screen yourself every load. Store kits often can't reach long or bending runs and can pack lint tighter, so a full professional cleaning is the safer choice.
